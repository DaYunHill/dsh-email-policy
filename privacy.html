Privacy Policy

dsh-email · Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who this applies to

dsh-email is a private, single-user desktop tool. The only person whose data it touches is the person who installed it and authorized it — the account owner. There are no other users, no accounts to register, and no service operated on behalf of anyone else.

2. What data is accessed

  • Email messages in the owner's own Gmail mailbox (read via IMAP).
  • The ability to send email from the owner's own Gmail address (via the Gmail API).
  • OAuth credentials issued by Google for the scope https://mail.google.com/.

3. Where data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens are written to a local file on that machine. Message content is processed in memory on that machine. No data is uploaded to any server operated by the author of this tool, and no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting is collected.

4. What is never done

  • No email content is sold, shared, published or transferred to third parties.
  • No email content is used to train machine-learning models.
  • No advertising, profiling or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to any Google account other than the one that explicitly granted consent.

5. Third parties

The only external service contacted is Google's own API infrastructure (Gmail API, OAuth 2.0 endpoints), strictly to perform the operations the owner requested. Google's own privacy policy applies to that traffic.

6. Revoking access

The owner can revoke this application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the local token file. Revocation takes effect immediately and no further requests will succeed.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local token files can be deleted at any time by the owner; doing so removes all credentials this tool holds. No copy exists anywhere else.

8. Children

This tool is not directed at children and processes no data belonging to anyone but its owner.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
